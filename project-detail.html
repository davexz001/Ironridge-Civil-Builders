Engineering Scope

Site Challenge

High regional water tables and compressed tenant occupancy targets required early soil stabilization alongside concurrent structural steel erection and concrete panel casting.

Field Execution Strategy

Ironridge self performed 28,000 cubic yards of lime stabilization, erected 3,100 tons of structural steel ahead of schedule, and poured super flat interior warehouse slabs using laser screed crews.

Self Performed Package

Key Deliverables

  • 01 Deep lime stabilized subgrade and storm detention basin across 42 acres
  • 02 94 cast concrete tilt wall panels with insulated thermal core construction
  • 03 Heavy duty 10 inch reinforced concrete truck aprons and 86 dock positions
  • 04 Two story administrative and dispatch suite with full backup generator yard
