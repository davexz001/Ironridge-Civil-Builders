Field Standards

Mandatory Jobsite Protocols

Protocol 01

Stop Work Authority for All Personnel

Every craft technician, engineer, and trade partner holds unconditional authority to pause operations whenever site conditions deviate from approved safety plans.

Protocol 02

Daily Job Hazard Analysis

Crews sign shift specific hazard worksheets each morning covering crane swing radii, trench shoring depth, utility clearance, and fall protection tie offs.

Protocol 03

Critical Lift and Excavation Engineering

Tandem girder lifts, deep trench box installations, and energized utility crossings require stamped engineering review prior to mobilization.

Protocol 04

Subcontractor Prequalification

Trade partners must pass financial bonding review, three year EMR verification, and documented OSHA training audits before contract award.
