Careers and Workforce Development

Build Complex Infrastructure With Experienced Craft and Engineering Teams

Ironridge Civil Builders offers structured advancement across active Texas and regional project sites as well as our Houston corporate headquarters.

Track 01

Field Operations Track

Designed for superintendents, structural concrete foremen, crane riggers, heavy equipment operators, and site safety coordinators leading daily jobsite execution.

  • Paid OSHA 30, NCCCO, and ACI certification renewals
  • Travel subsistence and fleet vehicle allowances for regional sites
  • Clear progression from Lead Foreman to General Superintendent
Track 02

Engineering and Office Track

Built for civil estimators, project managers, BIM coordinators, scheduling engineers, and contract administrators based at our Westpark Drive office in Houston.

  • Mentorship alongside senior preconstruction executives
  • PE licensure support and continuing technical coursework
  • Full medical, dental, and 401k matching benefits from day one
